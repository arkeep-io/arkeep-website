## Overview

Arkeep's site reads like a calm, trustworthy infrastructure tool for people who run their own servers. It has two halves in one Astro project: the marketing pages (`/`) and the Starlight documentation (`/docs/`). Both use the same token set (`src/styles/tokens.css`), so they look like one product.

**Palette.**
- The canvas is a **warm off-white** (`{colors.canvas}`, #fefbf9).
- A deep warm charcoal (`{colors.ink}`, #17100c) carries display and body type alike.
- The single brand voltage is **Arkeep orange** (`{colors.brand}`, #f66921), the hue the dashboard uses for primary actions, focus rings and active navigation.
- On the light theme, buttons and inline links use a deeper, AA-safe shade (`{colors.primary}`, #c94700), so white labels stay legible.

**Type.** **Be Vietnam Pro** is the single sans family (display 600, body 400). **JetBrains Mono** appears only in real code. Both are self-hosted; the site makes no third-party font calls, matching the product's stance.

**Themes.** The site ships **light and dark** themes from one token set, mirroring the dashboard. The theme follows `prefers-color-scheme` until the visitor chooses one. The choice is stored under Starlight's key (`starlight-theme`), so it holds across `/` and `/docs/`. Every neutral is tinted warm (hue 50–60 in OKLCH); there is no pure gray and no pure black.

**Signature.** The product performs on the page. Instead of screenshots in a browser frame, the homepage puts small, true, working fragments of Arkeep in front of the visitor:
- the hero backs up the page itself, lets you break it, and restores it;
- the retention panel applies restic's `forget` rules live;
- the agents table changes state as you scroll.

Real screenshots of the GUI (generated by `scripts/screenshots/`) appear cropped at true size, never shrunk into a frame.

**Key characteristics:**
- Flat warm surfaces separated by tone steps. No gradients, no shadows at rest, no hairline dividers outside product fragments.
- One action colour: orange. Teal, yellow and violet live only in product data (charts, status dots).
- One radius family from the dashboard (`--radius: 0.4rem`): 6px controls, 10px panels.
- Sentence case everywhere. No eyebrows, no pill badges, no index numbers.
- Headlines two lines or fewer at 1440, 768 and 390px.
- 120–200px between sections.

## Typography

- **Families.** `--font-sans`: "Be Vietnam Pro", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif. `--font-mono`: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace.
- **Loading.** Self-hosted via `@fontsource`, latin and latin-ext only. Weights 400, 500, 600, plus 400 italic. 400 and 600 latin are preloaded.

Five visible steps outside product fragments:

| Token | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--step-display` | clamp(40px → 84px) (hero h1 uses 4.6vw, 66px at 1440) | Hero headline, 600, −0.04em, leading 0.98 |
| `--step-h2` | clamp(28px → 48px) | Section headings, 600, −0.03em |
| `--step-lead` | clamp(20px → 26px) | Statements, feature titles, counts, footer lead |
| `--step-body` | 17px | Running text, 1.55 leading, +0.01em |
| `--step-small` | 14px | Notes, meta, buttons |

Inside `data-ui` fragments, text keeps the product's true sizes (13–15px). The footer wordmark is the one oversized moment (about 13vw).

**Principles.**
- Display weight is 600; Be Vietnam Pro gets heavy fast above that.
- Headlines are authored in two lines (`<span class="line">`), with `text-wrap: balance` on all headings.
- Monospace appears only in real `<pre>`/`<code>`, marked `translate="no"`.
- Typography uses curly quotes and apostrophes (’), the ellipsis character (…) and tabular numbers for counts.

## Layout

- **Container.** `.wrap` caps at 1360px with `--margin` (20–40px) side padding.
- **Section rhythm.** `--section-gap` (96–200px) between sections, clearly larger than any gap inside one.
- **Variety.** Neighbouring sections never share a composition:

| Section | Composition |
|---|---|
| Hero | 50/50 tone split, full-bleed |
| Retention | 62/38 panel |
| How it works | 5 + 6 of 12 columns, sticky stage |
| Features | 2-column checkerboard |
| Proof | One quiet row |
| Roadmap | Tabs plus bands |
| Install | One sentence |
| Footer | Tiles |

- **Phones.** Every section has its own phone composition: stacked hero (copy first), sticky stage at 46svh, crops that keep true size and run off the cell edge, and sticky roadmap tabs.

## Elevation & depth

- Surfaces are separated by a **tone step**, never by a line or a shadow.
- There are no shadows at rest.
- Product fragments (`data-ui`) keep the product's own 1px borders and radii; they are the only bordered boxes on marketing pages.

### Homepage sections (`src/components/home/`)

**`Hero`**
- Layout: two tones split at 50% (`--canvas` / `--surface-strong`).
- Copy (left):
  - two-line headline "Backups you can actually restore";
  - one lead sentence;
  - "Install Arkeep" button;
  - "Star on GitHub" link.
- Right half: the **`BackupFragment`**:
  - **Run now** streams a restic-shaped log built from the page's real sections (bytes measured from the DOM) and saves a snapshot whose ID is a SHA-256 of the content;
  - **Break something** deletes the hero copy;
  - **Restore** brings it back.
- Without JS it shows a static completed run.

**`Showcase`** (retention)
- Panel split 62/38.
- Destination radios for NAS, B2 and SFTP, each with its own rules.
- A 30-day × 4-runs timeline (kept = filled orange, forgotten = outline).
- Live count "Keeps N of 120 snapshots".
- Steppers for last, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and yearly.
- Logic: `src/lib/retention.ts`, mirroring restic's `ApplyPolicy`.

**`HowItWorks`**
- Three statements: "Start the server." → "Then enroll an agent on each machine." → "Policies run everywhere, on schedule."
- Beside them, a sticky Agents fragment in three states: empty, one agent connecting, four online with a backup running.
- Without JS the three states stack.

**`Features`**
- Two-column checkerboard of six cells (text-top / fragment-top alternating by row).
- Fragments are either HTML product fragments (Docker volume picker, command-source table) or `ScreenCrop`s of real screenshots, cropped by a rectangle in interface pixels.
- One muted loop of a running job, with a Pause/Play control. It does not play under reduced motion.

**`Proof`**
- One quiet row: "Built on Restic and Rclone. Writes to" followed by destination names set in type (no logos).
- Then a real `restic -r … snapshots` command: "Your snapshots stay readable without Arkeep."

**`Roadmap`**
- WAI-ARIA version tabs (v1.0, v1.x, v2.0, v2.x).
- Alternating tone bands with "Shipped" or "Planned" in words.
- Data from the arkeep README (`src/lib/roadmap.ts`).

**`InstallCta`**
- The sentence "Back up your [platform ▾] tonight.": the select is the blank and "tonight." is the submit button.
- It leads to the matching install guide and shows that guide's first command, with a Copy button.

## Motion

**Signature.** The hero headline rises line by line from behind its baseline (900ms, strong ease-out, second line 110ms later). The lead and actions then fade in.

**Entrances.** Each section's heading, then its text, then its fragment, rise 24px with opacity, once, staggered 60–90ms (`[data-reveal]`, `src/scripts/reveal.ts`).

**Feedback.** Every button, tab, link, tile and stepper has hover and press states.

**Rules.**
- Transform and opacity only.
- Everything has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.
- Content is visible without JS, because hidden states only apply under the `.js` class.

## House rules

These come from taste-skill and are checked by `lint.mjs` and `inspect.mjs`:
- Headlines two lines or fewer at every width.
- No eyebrows, pill badges or index numbering (01/02).
- No gradients, glass, glows or shadows at rest.
- No thin rules or 1px boxes outside real tables, form fields and `data-ui` fragments.
- Monospace only in real code.
- No icon-topped card rows, no emoji, no styled accent word in a headline.
- Two typefaces at most.
- One action colour.

A rule broken on purpose carries an inline `taste-skill-ignore` comment with the reason.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Use `--primary` for anything white text sits on, and for orange text on the light canvas.
- Keep neutrals warm and pull every value from tokens; never inline hex in components.
- Show the product with true-size fragments or true-size screenshot crops, marked `data-ui`.
- Write sentence-case, specific labels ("Install Arkeep", "Suggest a feature"), with no generic "Get started".
- Keep both themes working and swap screenshots with the theme.

### Don't
- Don't put white text on #f66921 (3.0:1).
- Don't add a second action colour, a gradient, or a shadow at rest.
- Don't shrink whole-window screenshots into frames.
- Don't load fonts or assets from third-party CDNs.
- Don't claim what the product doesn't do. For example, the GUI shows live **progress**; logs arrive after completion.

## Known gaps

- **Pictures.** The homepage has no photographs of the world around the product, only interface fragments and one job recording. taste-skill warns about this.
  - Candidate next step: one archival engraving of an ark, from open collections, as the footer or proof motif. It would tie into "Your infra's Ark".
- **Screenshot data.** The demo dataset is small: one day of history and three agents. The dashboard crops would benefit from a longer seeded history.
- **Docs pages.** On docs pages, Starlight's own chrome (sidebar rules, pagination shadows) does not follow the marketing house rules. This is intentional.
- **Video loop.** The loop starts with one blank frame from page load. The poster frame covers it until playback begins.
