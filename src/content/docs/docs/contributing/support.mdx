Arkeep is maintained independently by Filippo.

It is built around a simple idea: before touching production, you should know you have a backup you can
actually recover from.

If Arkeep saves you time, helps your workflow or gives you more confidence in your infrastructure, you can
support the work behind it through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/arkeep-io). Sponsoring helps
fund bug fixes, documentation, releases, restore testing, and the next pieces of the roadmap, from inventory
to safer update orchestration.

Thank you to everyone who has sponsored Arkeep, past and present. Your support keeps the project independent
and moving forward.

## Other ways to help

- Star the [repository](https://github.com/arkeep-io/arkeep) and tell others about it.
- Report bugs and suggest features in the [issues](https://github.com/arkeep-io/arkeep/issues).
- Improve the documentation or the code: see [Contributing](/docs/contributing/).
